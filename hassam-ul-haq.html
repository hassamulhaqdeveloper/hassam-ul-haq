FAQ

Before you ask.

Something missing? Send me a brief and I'll answer directly.

How long does a project take?

It depends on scope. After the Brief and Blueprint stages I give you a clear timeline with milestones.

Do I need technical knowledge?

No. I explain decisions in plain language and document what I build so your team can use it.

Will I understand what you built?

Yes. Handoff is part of the work: the system is documented and walked through before launch.

How is pricing decided?

By scope: what needs to be built, which systems connect, and what support you need. You get a quote before work starts.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes. I work remotely and communicate asynchronously with scheduled calls when needed.
